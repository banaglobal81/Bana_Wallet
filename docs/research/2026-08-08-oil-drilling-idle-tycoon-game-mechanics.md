# What mechanics from resource-mining / oil-drilling idle & tycoon games are proven and popular, and which could transfer to a staking-as-oil-drilling reskin?

Retrieved 2026-08-08. All sources listed at the bottom with retrieval date.

## Summary

The idle/tycoon mining genre has well-documented, reusable patterns: a short "choke point" core loop (produce → transport/process → sell → reinvest), offline/idle earning with a capped catch-up window, exponential-cost/linear-or-log-reward progression curves, and prestige (soft-reset-for-permanent-multiplier) systems that are the genre's primary long-term retention lever. Kolibri Games' **Idle Miner Tycoon** is the best-documented named success (100M+ downloads, 75% Day-1 retention at launch vs. ~40% category norm), built around a four-stage core loop with three intentional "choke points" that double as the monetization surface. **Egg Inc**'s Soul Egg prestige system and **Deep Town**'s multi-resource excavation loop are the other most-cited reference points; the Web3-native precedent, **CryptoMines (Legacy → Reborn)**, is a documented cautionary tale — its token-emission-as-mining design hyperinflated and collapsed (ETERNAL token lost ~99% of value within days), which is directly relevant as a "don't" for a real-money-adjacent product. Genre-wide, offline earnings are deliberately capped (by time or amount) specifically to force return visits, and reward-schedule/streak/milestone design is well-studied territory — some of it (variable-ratio "random drop" reward schedules, loot-box-style mechanics) is explicitly flagged in the design literature as a reinforcement pattern to be cautious with. The one named example in the original prompt, "PIT — People In Trouble," could not be verified as an existing title in any source consulted and is flagged as an open question rather than reported as fact.

## Findings

### 1. Named examples

- **Idle Miner Tycoon (Kolibri Games, 2016)** is the most heavily documented success in this genre. It surpassed 100M+ downloads and Kolibri states 150M+ downloads across their titles; the original build reached **75% Day-1 retention**, well above the ~40% D1 retention typical for free-to-play mobile games at the time. · confidence: high · [kolibrigames.com/press](https://www.kolibrigames.com/press/kolibri-games-hit-title-idle-miner-tycoon-surpasses-150-million-downloads/), [gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game-eight-lessons-from-kolibri-games), [kolibrigames.com/blog/celebrating-five-years](https://www.kolibrigames.com/blog/celebrating-five-years-of-idle-miner-tycoon-and-still-going-strong/)
- Its core loop has **four stages — Mine, Transport, Sell, Upgrade — with three deliberate "choke points"** between them (mine output vs. elevator/transport throughput vs. warehouse/sell throughput). Kolibri's own retrospective states these choke points **are** the monetization surface: whichever stage is the bottleneck is what a player will pay to unblock. Playtesting found many players over-invested in the mine stage and neglected the elevator, creating unintended stalls. · confidence: high (Kolibri's own account) · [gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game-eight-lessons-from-kolibri-games)
- Kolibri credits part of the game's distinctiveness to the *digging-down* framing (versus building upward, the norm for tycoon games at the time) plus strong art/character direction and a live-ops cadence of weekly updates and event mines. · confidence: medium (source is the developer's own retrospective, not independently verified) · [gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game-eight-lessons-from-kolibri-games)

- **Oil Tycoon 2 (Holy Cow Studio)** and its lineage (**Oil Tycoon 2: Idle Empire**, **Idle Oil Empire**, **Oil Era**, **Oil Rig Tycoon**, **Oil Tycoon Idle 3D**) are the closest genre-mapped comps to an oil theme specifically. Core loop: tap/drill oil → truck it to a refinery or hold in storage for better prices → hire staff and buy equipment tiers → reinvest into more/better rigs. The "hold oil in storage waiting for a better sale price" mechanic is a distinctive market-timing sub-loop not present in most mining idle games. · confidence: medium (app-store/store-listing descriptions, not independently reviewed) · [apps.apple.com/oil-tycoon-2](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/oil-tycoon-2-idle-empire-game/id1441938955), [play.google.com/oil-tycoon-2](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.holycowstudio.oiltycoon2&hl=en&gl=US)

- **Deep Town: Mining Factory / Idle Mining Tycoon (Rockbite Games)** combines active digging (a mining laser uncovers new areas), idle machine placement, a resource-processing/crafting chain, and light narrative/exploration content (archaeological finds, monster encounters at depth). Idle progress is explicitly described as happening "at a far slower rate than active progress" — i.e. idle time is a floor, not the primary loop. No public source consulted describes a prestige/rebirth system for this title; this is reported as unconfirmed rather than absent. · confidence: medium · [tvtropes.org/DeepTownMiningFactory](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DeepTownMiningFactory), [play.google.com/deeptown](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rockbite.deeptown&hl=en_US)

It was returned by search in place of the "Mr. Digger" title named in the prompt — no source consulted contains a game called "Mr. Digger"; this is flagged below as an open question rather than substituted silently. · confidence: medium (developer/publisher blog, not third-party reviewed) · [blog.mrmine.com/drilling-games-mr-mine-beginners-guide](https://blog.mrmine.com/drilling-games-mr-mine-beginners-guide/)

- **Egg Inc (Auxbrain, 2016)** is the most-cited reference for prestige-loop design outside pure mining themes but is structurally identical (linear resource accumulation → tiered production buildings → prestige reset). Its "Soul Egg" system: prestige unlocks after the first Soul Egg is earned; prestiging resets population, common research, habitats, silos, vehicles and egg tier, but **preserves** permit, Epic Research, the Hyperloop Station, Golden Eggs, Piggy Bank level, boosts, Mystical Eggs and Artifacts. Soul Eggs give a flat **+10% permanent earnings multiplier each**, and the number earned per prestige scales with cumulative earnings ("Bocks") since the last prestige. · confidence: high (fan wiki cross-referenced against multiple independent pages) · [egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/Prestige](https://egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/Prestige), [egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/Earnings_Bonus](https://egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/Earnings_Bonus/Soul_Eggs), [tap-guides.com/egg-inc-prestige-soul-eggs-guide](https://tap-guides.com/2025/10/24/egg-inc-prestige-soul-eggs-guide/)

### 2. Core loop patterns

- The dominant minute-to-minute loop across the genre is **produce → move/process → sell → reinvest**, with each stage individually upgradable and deliberately bottlenecked against the others (Idle Miner Tycoon's "3 choke points" being the clearest documented instance). This creates a self-explanatory loop playable "on a 10-minute train journey." · confidence: high · [gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game-eight-lessons-from-kolibri-games)
- Day-to-day loop layers on top: log in → collect idle/offline accumulation → spend on upgrades → check event/live-ops content → log out; offline production continues (capped) between sessions. · confidence: high, this pattern recurs across every title profiled above · [dracoarts.com/why-idle-mechanics-work](https://dracoarts.com/blogs/why-idle-mechanics-work-even-when-you-re-offline-the-science-of-passive-progress), [games.themindstudios.com/idle-clicker-game-design](https://games.themindstudios.com/post/idle-clicker-game-design-and-monetization/)
- Active-tap variants (Oil Tycoon 2, Deep Town) layer a manual tap/click action on top of the idle base rate — tapping accelerates but does not replace idle production. · confidence: medium · [apps.apple.com/oil-tycoon-2](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/oil-tycoon-2-idle-empire-game/id1441938955), [tvtropes.org/DeepTownMiningFactory](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DeepTownMiningFactory)

### 3. Progression & leveling systems

- Idle-game math conventionally splits into two curves: **upgrade cost grows exponentially** (commonly a 1.1–1.25x multiplier per purchase) while **reward/output grows linearly or logarithmically**. A 1.10x growth factor "feels comfortable"; pushing toward 1.25x makes late-game costs explode, which is a deliberate lever for pacing when players hit a wall (and, per Idle Miner Tycoon's model, when they're offered a paid unblock). · confidence: medium (game-design literature/tooling, not a single authoritative primary source) · [designthegame.com/example-level-curve-formulas](https://www.designthegame.com/learning/courses/course/fundamentals-level-curve-design/example-level-curve-formulas-game-progression), [gamedeveloper.com/balancing-tips-idle-idol](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/balancing-tips-how-we-managed-math-on-idle-idol)
- Curve *shape* is chosen to match pacing goals: exponential curves create a late-game wall (used where devs want a hard prestige trigger); linear curves flatten pacing (used to prevent infinite grinding, e.g., subscription MMOs); log curves front-load fast early levels and taper — a common idle-game choice for the crucial first-session hook. · confidence: medium · [designthegame.com](https://www.designthegame.com/learning/courses/course/fundamentals-level-curve-design/example-level-curve-formulas-game-progression)
- Equipment/rig tiers escalate by unlocking new production nodes or new zones/mineshafts rather than only multiplying existing ones (Mr. Mine's depth-based zone unlocks; Idle Miner Tycoon's per-mine elevator/warehouse tiers) — this keeps the *visual* representation of growth (new site, new rig) synchronized with the *numeric* growth, which the genre relies on to make abstract multipliers legible. · confidence: medium · [blog.mrmine.com](https://blog.mrmine.com/drilling-games-mr-mine-beginners-guide/), [gameanalytics.com](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game-eight-lessons-from-kolibri-games)

### 4. Prestige/rebirth mechanics

- Prestige systems are near-universal in this genre and exist specifically to solve the "stagnation at level cap" problem: instead of the player's progress plateauing, a voluntary reset in exchange for a permanent multiplier restarts short-term progression while compounding long-term power, which is described in the literature as "grind, reset, grind faster, repeat." · confidence: medium-high (multiple independent design-blog sources converge on this framing) · [sixsevenz.com/prestige-systems-explained](https://sixsevenz.com/prestige-systems-explained-free-player-restart-guide/), [gtstu.com/game-progression-system-design](https://gtstu.com/game-progression-system-design-keep-players-hooked/), [moddingtree.com/guide/prestige](https://moddingtree.com/guide/recipes/prestige)
- Egg Inc's Soul Egg system is the clearest documented what-resets-what pattern: **resets** population/common research/habitats/silos/vehicles/egg tier; **keeps** permit, epic research, premium-adjacent unlocks, and all previously earned prestige currency. The permanent multiplier (+10%/Soul Egg) is scaled to cumulative earnings since the last prestige, which incentivizes timing a prestige for maximum yield rather than resetting arbitrarily. · confidence: high · [egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/Prestige](https://egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/Prestige)
- Prestige is also used as an inflation/power-creep control valve at the game-economy level (cited example: Path of Exile's seasonal league resets), separate from its retention function — i.e., resets can serve economy health, not just player psychology. · confidence: medium · [gtstu.com](https://gtstu.com/game-progression-system-design-keep-players-hooked/)

### 5. Automation & idle mechanics

- Offline/idle earning exists specifically to give players a reason to feel the game "kept going" while away, but every credible source describes it as **deliberately capped** — either by a maximum elapsed time (e.g., 4h, 24h) or a maximum accumulated amount — precisely so that accumulation *stops* and creates a reason to return. Uncapped idle earning would remove the return incentive entirely. · confidence: high (converges across multiple independent design-blog sources) · [themindstudios.com/idle-clicker-game-design](https://games.themindstudios.com/post/idle-clicker-game-design-and-monetization/), [enterprisepeak.co.uk/idle-games](https://enterprisepeak.co.uk/idle-games/)
- Return-cadence pacing is commonly tiered by resource type within a single game — e.g., one cited implementation caps a fast resource every ~20 minutes, a mid-tier resource every ~5 hours, and a slow "big" resource every ~2 days — layering multiple "come back" windows so there's always a near-term and a long-term reason to return. · confidence: medium (single source, not cross-verified against a named title) · [enterprisepeak.co.uk/idle-games](https://enterprisepeak.co.uk/idle-games/)
- Technically, offline-earning calculations must be **server-time-anchored**, not client-clock-anchored, specifically to prevent players manipulating device clocks to farm offline rewards; production idle games move the "welcome back" calculation to the backend for this reason. · confidence: high (independent engineering write-up, consistent with standard anti-cheat practice) · [edvins.io/rebuilding-welcome-back-mechanic](https://edvins.io/rebuilding-the-welcome-back-mechanic-from-idle-games-in-react)

### 6. Visual/thematic patterns specific to oil drilling

- The genre consistently converts abstract accumulation into **physical, fillable containers** — barrels with visible fill state (e.g., a color-changing ring: red = not full, green = full in one Roblox oil-drill implementation) are the standard way of making "you have X units" legible without reading a number. · confidence: medium (single concrete example found, but pattern is consistent with barrel/tank UI conventions across the reviewed titles) · [space-mining-tycoon.fandom.com/Oil_Drill](https://space-mining-tycoon-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Oil_Drill)
- **Site/rig escalation is the primary "growth is visible" mechanic**: a large purchasable derrick (one example: $750,000 for a rig producing $100,000/600s) represents a step-change in visual scale and output simultaneously, rather than a same-asset numeric-only upgrade. Later-tier titles add refineries as a distinct processing stage downstream of the well (raw fluid → refined product), giving a second, visually distinct progression track beyond "more wells." · confidence: medium · [noob-army-tycoon.fandom.com/Oil_Rig](https://noob-army-tycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Oil_Rig_(Tycoon)), [higherplaingames.com/drill-deal-oil-tycoon-review](https://higherplaingames.com/pc/drill-deal-oil-tycoon-review/)
- Map/site expansion (moving from a single well to a field, then to multiple fields/regions) is the standard way these games represent horizontal (not just vertical/numeric) growth — cited explicitly in Oil Tycoon 2's own positioning ("from a single oil drill into a million dollar all-round oil extraction platform on sea") and Mr. Mine's depth-zone system. · confidence: medium (marketing copy + game-blog description, not independently verified gameplay) · [apps.apple.com/oil-tycoon-2](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/oil-tycoon-2-idle-empire-game/id1441938955), [blog.mrmine.com](https://blog.mrmine.com/drilling-games-mr-mine-beginners-guide/)

### 7. What drives "addictiveness"

- The design literature explicitly names **variable-ratio reward schedules** (random-value drops, loot boxes) as the same reinforcement principle used in gambling machines — uncertainty about the next reward's size/timing is what sustains engagement, independent of the reward's average value. This is flagged in the literature itself as ethically fraught, not merely as a neutral mechanic. · confidence: high (academic/ACM and deceptive-design literature converge) · [dl.acm.org/10.1145/3701571.3701604](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3701571.3701604), [deceptive.design/chapter-11](https://deceptive.design/book/contents/chapter-11/)
- **Streaks** are a separately-studied, lower-risk retention mechanic (loss-aversion framed, not chance-based): breaking a streak is felt as a loss, and sources cite users being markedly more likely to return daily once a streak (commonly 7+ days) is established. The same literature flags an ethical failure mode — streaks becoming a source of anxiety/obligation rather than reward — and recommends mitigations such as "streak freeze" and "earn-back" mechanics that don't require payment. · confidence: medium (single aggregator source citing an unverified "2.3x" multiplier; the multiplier itself is not independently confirmed) · [appstorys.com/streaks-milestones](https://appstorys.com/blog-Streaks-Milestones-Habit-Gamification), [uxmag.medium.com/psychology-of-hot-streak-game-design](https://uxmag.medium.com/the-psychology-of-hot-streak-game-design-how-to-keep-players-coming-back-every-day-without-shame-3dde153f239c)
- **Milestone celebrations** (distinct, non-repeating animation/reward at fixed thresholds) are cited as compounding with streaks — one source claims 40–60% higher DAU when milestones and streaks are combined versus either alone, and separately warns that if the *first* meaningful milestone is set too far out (e.g., 30 days), new users disengage before reaching it, arguing for an early quick-win milestone. · confidence: medium (single aggregator source; percentage not independently corroborated) · [yukaichou.com/milestone-unlocks](https://yukaichou.com/advanced-gamification/the-power-of-milestone-unlocks-in-gamification-design/), [appstorys.com](https://appstorys.com/blog-Streaks-Milestones-Habit-Gamification)
- Dark-pattern research broadly notes that mobile games (not only gambling apps) use interface design to obscure time/money spent while maximizing engagement via social-comparison and variable-reward mechanics, and that these patterns appear even in titles perceived as "benign" — relevant caution for a wallet product where "engagement" mechanics sit adjacent to real money. · confidence: medium (survey/ACM-adjacent literature, general claim not tied to a specific title in this genre) · [dl.acm.org/10.1145/3701571.3701604](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3701571.3701604)

### 8. Team/co-op or social elements

- Social/competitive layers are common but usually additive, not core-loop-required: **leaderboards** (global and friends-only, ranked by net worth or growth rate), **guilds/clans** with shared goals or cooperative income boosts, and **time-limited competitive events** are all cited as standard genre features layered on top of the solo economic loop. · confidence: medium (aggregator/blog source, not primary developer documentation for most claims) · [blog.popcorngames.io/idle-tycoon-games-overview](https://blog.popcorngames.io/idle-tycoon-games-overview/)
- Idle Miner Tycoon specifically is cited as supporting friend expeditions and leaderboard competition, plus social sharing to Facebook/Reddit/Discord communities — an explicit acquisition/retention loop via external community, not just in-app social features. · confidence: medium (source is a secondary aggregator summarizing the game's Social Features wiki, not the wiki page itself directly cited) · [idleminertycoon.fandom.com/Social_Features](https://idleminertycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Social_Features)
- Guild-centric titles (Guildmaker Tycoon, Idle Guild: Hero Tycoon) push further into shared-goal cooperative mechanics (e.g., co-op wall defense) layered onto an idle economic base — evidence that referral/rank-adjacent social structures are a proven, not novel, extension of this genre. · confidence: low-medium (store-listing descriptions only) · [play.google.com/guildmaker-tycoon](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=manager.rpg.pub.empire&hl=en_US)

## Contradictions

1. **Does Deep Town have a prestige/rebirth system?** No source consulted confirms or denies this explicitly. TVTropes and the Play Store listing describe a rich excavation/crafting/automation loop but are silent on end-game reset mechanics, while every other major title profiled (Idle Miner Tycoon, Egg Inc) explicitly documents one. This is reported as unconfirmed, not assumed absent.

2. **Is CryptoMines Reborn a "structurally similar precedent" in the way the prompt frames it, or a cautionary counter-example?** The prompt frames CryptoMines Reborn as "structurally similar to what we're doing" (token emission gamified as mining). The evidence gathered shows the *original* CryptoMines game's core failure mode — token emission directly tied to in-game mining/production with insufficient sinks, causing hyperinflation and a ~99% token-value collapse — is a genuine structural parallel worth naming as a risk, not a template to imitate. No source consulted confirms whether "Reborn" (the redesigned version) achieved lasting stability; treat its outcome as unresolved rather than as evidence the redesign succeeded.

3. **Streak/milestone engagement-lift percentages (the cited "2.3x" and "40–60% higher DAU" figures)** come from single aggregator sources (Medium, appstorys.com) without an underlying study or named methodology. They are reported here as claims in the secondary literature, not as verified statistics, and should not be treated as benchmarks BANA can expect to replicate.

## Mechanics most applicable to a staking-as-oil-drilling reskin

This section synthesizes findings above against the stated constraint: BANA's underlying system is **fixed-term locked deposits with daily interest**, not free-form tap-to-earn extraction. This is a findings synthesis, not a design decision — `pm` / `game-planner` own what BANA actually builds.

1. **Choke-point core loop, reframed as a legibility layer, not a new economy.** Idle Miner Tycoon's produce → transport → sell → reinvest loop with visible bottlenecks is a UI/visualization pattern (rig drilling → pipeline flow → refinery output → barrel counter) that can sit *on top of* a real fixed-term/daily-interest schedule without inventing new extraction mechanics — the "choke point" is simply the real lock duration and real daily accrual rate, visualized as flow, not gated by pay-to-unblock levers (which would be inappropriate for a real-money product). Source basis: [gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game-eight-lessons-from-kolibri-games).

2. **Capped, server-time-anchored "return to collect" rhythm** — but tied to the real daily interest accrual event, not an arbitrary idle timer. The genre's core insight (offline earning must be capped and must return a player) maps directly onto "your daily interest posts, come see your barrel fill" — no invented mechanic needed, since BANA's real staking product already has a daily cadence. Source basis: [themindstudios.com/idle-clicker-game-design](https://games.themindstudios.com/post/idle-clicker-game-design-and-monetization/), [edvins.io/rebuilding-welcome-back-mechanic](https://edvins.io/rebuilding-the-welcome-back-mechanic-from-idle-games-in-react).

3. **Physical/fillable-container visualization for accrual** (barrel fill-state, derrick scale, refinery throughput) as a direct reskin of "principal + accrued interest," since the genre's core visual trick — converting a number into a container that visibly fills — requires no new financial mechanic, only a rendering layer over real balances. Source basis: [space-mining-tycoon.fandom.com/Oil_Drill](https://space-mining-tycoon-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Oil_Drill), [noob-army-tycoon.fandom.com/Oil_Rig](https://noob-army-tycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Oil_Rig_(Tycoon)).

4. **Tier/rig escalation mapped to real term-length or real principal tiers**, not arbitrary unlocks — i.e., a bigger locked amount or longer term unlocks a visually bigger rig/derrick, which is a direct reskin of the genre's "equipment tier" progression without introducing any gameplay-driven yield boost that isn't backed by the real product's actual terms. Source basis: [blog.mrmine.com](https://blog.mrmine.com/drilling-games-mr-mine-beginners-guide/), [gameanalytics.com](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/making-a-hit-idle-game-eight-lessons-from-kolibri-games).

5. **Milestone celebration at term-completion / rollover, not chance-based reward.** The literature's milestone-celebration pattern (fixed, predictable, threshold-based, non-random) is the safe half of the genre's reward-schedule toolkit — it maps cleanly onto "your fixed-term stake matured, here's your animation," with the explicit exclusion of the *variable-ratio* reward pattern (random-size drops, loot-box-style payouts), which the design literature itself flags as a reinforcement pattern to be cautious with. Source basis: [yukaichou.com/milestone-unlocks](https://yukaichou.com/advanced-gamification/the-power-of-milestone-unlocks-in-gamification-design/), [dl.acm.org/10.1145/3701571.3701604](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3701571.3701604), [deceptive.design/chapter-11](https://deceptive.design/book/contents/chapter-11/).

6. **Prestige-style reframing as re-staking, not a destructive reset.** The genre's prestige loop (reset short-term state, keep a permanent multiplier) doesn't map cleanly to a real fixed-term deposit — BANA cannot "reset" a user's real principal for a game bonus without that bonus being an actual, funded product feature (e.g., a real loyalty-tier rate improvement for repeat staking), which is a `pm` product-policy decision, not something this reskin can synthesize for free the way a pure-game economy can. Flagging this as the mechanic that transfers *least* cleanly and needs explicit design/finance sign-off before any "rebirth for permanent bonus" framing is used, since in this genre prestige currency is fictional and free to mint, whereas any BANA-side yield boost is a real financial liability. Source basis: [egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/Prestige](https://egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/Prestige), [sixsevenz.com/prestige-systems-explained](https://sixsevenz.com/prestige-systems-explained-free-player-restart-guide/).

Social/leaderboard elements (finding 8) and CryptoMines' collapse (finding 1) are not included above as "transferable mechanics" but are directly relevant to `pm`/`game-planner` scoping: leaderboards/guild-style visuals could echo BANA's existing referral/rank system per the prompt's own framing, and CryptoMines is the clearest available warning against ever tying in-game "mining output" to token emission or synthetic yield rather than the real, funded interest rate.
